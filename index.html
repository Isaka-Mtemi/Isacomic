Stakeholders / Wahusika - Flow Process:
• TRA - Tanzania Revenue Authority: Tax assessment, import duty calculation, VAT, duty payment - most important flow stakeholder - assessment based on invoice
• TBS - Tanzania Bureau of Standards: Quality inspection, standards check, PVoC, permit for electronics, shoes, clothes, spare parts - quality flow
• TPA - Tanzania Ports Authority: Port storage charges, handling charges, port clearance - port flow
• Shipping Line: Bill of Lading release, container release, shipping charges - shipping flow
• Customs Broker (Us - ISACOMIC LOGISTICS): We handle all documentation, follow up with all stakeholders - we are flow managers

Documents Required - Flow Documents:
• Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading (BL), TBS Permit/PVoC, Import Permit, TRA Assessment, ID, TIN, Business License

Flow Process: We submit documents → TRA assessment → TBS inspection → Pay duty & port charges → Get release → Port outlet service - we take cargo out of port - flow continues to inland transport
